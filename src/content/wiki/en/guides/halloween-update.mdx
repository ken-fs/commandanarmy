The **Halloween update** is Command An Army's next patch. It's expected on **Saturday, October 31, 2026**. The big addition is **Zombie Mode**: a PvE mode where waves of zombies and bosses attack a base you have to defend.

Short version: **zombies don't have shields**. Level up one or two high-damage units and an archer line now, so you're ready on day one.

## When is the Halloween update?

**Saturday, October 31, 2026**, going by the creators who covered the official teaser on October 27. They said it was three days away. The current battle pass ends the same day.

There are no official patch notes yet, and no exact release time.

## What is Zombie Mode?

It's the first PvE mode in Command An Army. PvE means you fight the game's NPCs instead of other players.

Zombies come in waves and walk toward a base. The base has its own health, so you lose if they reach it and break it. Bosses show up between the regular hordes. Both creators who covered it compare it to tower defense.

The key detail: **zombies have no shields**. In normal matches, shields cut a lot of your damage. Here every hit lands in full, so raw damage matters more than usual.

## Best units to level before Zombie Mode

These picks come from creator breakdowns of the teaser. **Nobody has played the mode yet**, so this is prep, not a tested tier list.

| Role | Units | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main damage** | [Laestrygonian](/units/laestrygonian/), [Myrmidon](/units/myrmidon/), [Samurai](/units/samurai/), [Berserker](/units/berserker/), [Immortal](/units/immortal/) | Highest damage, and no shields means none of it is wasted. |
| **Ranged** | [Flame Archer](/units/archer/) | Hits from behind your line. The burn also lands in full on unshielded zombies. |
| **Base defense** | [Phalangite](/units/phalangite/), [Halberdier](/units/halberdier/), [Imperial Wall](/units/shieldman/) | Hold the line in front of the base. The Phalangite hits several zombies at once. |
| **Budget damage** | [Knight](/units/knight/) | Plays like a cheaper Myrmidon if you don't have one. |

Take away from this: **if you can only level one unit, make it a damage unit**. You want to kill each wave fast so zombies don't pile up on the base.

A few units look weaker here than usual:

- **[Javelineer](/units/javelineer/):** its big strength is piercing shields. Zombies don't have any, so that edge is gone.
- **[Spartan](/units/spartan/):** great at holding a point, but it doesn't kill fast. Useful only if the map has a narrow path to block.
- **[Lancer](/units/lancer/):** huge charge damage, but hordes keep coming. You'd have to time charges between waves. Worth testing, not counting on.

The [Berserker](/units/berserker/) is a good pick right now. The October 19 patch buffed it to 18 per squad and 33 damage.

## How to get ready before Saturday

1. **Pick one damage unit and level it.** The Laestrygonian if you have it. Otherwise a Myrmidon, Samurai or Berserker.
2. **Evolve an archer toward Flame Archer.** See the [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) for the path.
3. **Finish the current battle pass.** It ends October 31. Claim what you've earned before the update.
4. **Redeem every working code.** Free Gems mean more summons for whatever new units come with the update. The [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) lists them all.
