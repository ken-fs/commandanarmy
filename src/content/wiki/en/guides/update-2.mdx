## What Update 2 changed

Update 2 is the **Ranked & Guilds** patch. The Roblox page carries the `[GUILD & RANKED]` tag, which is the quickest way to confirm which build you are on.

Unlike the previous patches, Update 2 did not reshape the unit meta — it added game modes and two units. The unit meta itself was already changed by the **1.5 patch** that nerfed Archer range, buffed Spearmen and nerfed Spartans. If you are catching up on both, the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) covers the resulting rankings.

## Ranked mode

Ranked arrived with the patch, and the first week is the worst time to judge it — everyone is queuing at once, so early matches are against the strongest players online rather than a representative field.

It plays as **team versus team**, not one-on-one. If you were expecting a duel ladder, that is not what this is.

## Guilds

Guilds also shipped with Update 2, and the practical warning attached to them is cost: guild progression carries **Gem costs**, and creators describe them as expensive for what they return early on.

The sensible order is to settle your unit lineup first. Gems spent on a guild are Gems not spent on summons, and summons are what build the army that wins matches. The [star limit guide](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) explains why a small number of good units beats a large number of mediocre ones.

## The two new units

### Rapier → Rapier Knight

**Rapier** is a 2-star unit that evolves into **Rapier Knight**, and the reason it is interesting is the mechanic: the evolved form can **parry**.

Parry is a new kind of defensive tool for a mid-cost unit, and it is worth watching for one specific reason — the shield matchup. Command An Army's counter system revolves around block damage and shield breaking, so a unit that punishes incoming attacks is a different answer to the same problem. Whether it lands depends on how reliably parry triggers in a crowd.

Two practical notes before you invest:

- **Evolving it needs level 20.** That is a real time cost, so nobody has a tested Rapier Knight yet.
- **Evolving raises the star cost.** Rank up first, then evolve — the [star limit guide](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) explains why evolving too early can price a unit out of your own lineup.

### Reaper

**[Reaper](/units/reaper/)** is the other Update 2 unit, and the verdict is already in from creators who tested it: it is one of the weakest units in the game.

The design problem is conceptual rather than numerical. Reaper is built for **one-on-one fights** in a game that is fundamentally about army-versus-army pushes. It also loses to archers it cannot reach, which in the current meta — where Flame Archers are S tier — is close to fatal.

It sits in C tier on our [tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/). Do not spend summons chasing it.

## What to do with the patch

If you are new: the update did not change the beginner path. Finish the tutorial, redeem the [working codes](/codes/all-codes/) for Gems, and follow the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) — then decide on units with the [star limit](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) in mind.

If you already have a lineup: Ranked is worth queuing once the first-week rush settles, Guilds can wait until your units are sorted, and Rapier is a watch-list unit rather than a chase target until someone has tested a levelled Rapier Knight.

The meta changes on the 1.5-style patches, not the mode patches — so the [tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) is the page to recheck when the next unit drop lands.
