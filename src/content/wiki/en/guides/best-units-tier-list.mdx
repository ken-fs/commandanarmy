This tier list reflects **creator consensus after Update 2** (Ranked & Guilds) and the **1.5 balance patch**, as of September 2026. There is no official ranking in Command An Army, and the meta moves with every patch — this page is updated when it does.

Two patches reshaped this list. **1.5 nerfed Archer range hard**, which removed the worst matchup for every melee unit and pushed Berserkers up. The same patch **buffed Spearmen** (faster, four more units in formation) and **nerfed Spartans** (they now break formation when hit). Update 2 then added Ranked, Guilds and two new units: **Rapier** and **Reaper**.

If you are still learning the systems behind the rankings, read [unit counters](/guides/unit-counters/) first — a well-countered S-tier unit still loses to a C-tier unit that hard-counters it.

## S tier

| Unit | Type | Why it's S-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spartan** | Special (4★) | Still the definitive frontline even after 1.5 nerfed them. Creators describe the shield as taking "three to five business days" to break while the unit shreds anything in front of it. The nerf is real — they now break formation when hit — but nothing else tanks like this. |
| **Berserker** | Special (4★) | The biggest winner of the 1.5 patch. With archer range cut, the unit that used to melt before reaching the line now gets there. It still loses to Flame Archers unless it charges them directly. |
| **Flame Archer** | Ranged (3★) | The best ranged unit in the game and the payoff of the Archer line. Arched shots let you place them on high ground, and everything they hit ignites. Creators call it a must-have and the reason to invest in Archers at all. |
| **Lancer** | Cavalry (4★) | The fastest unit in the game with the highest rush damage. Its job is shock — breaking a line in one charge. It does not win prolonged fights, which is why creators pair it with Immortals for the follow-up. |

## A tier

| Unit | Type | Why it's A-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Halberdier** | Spear (3★) | The versatile pick: stops cavalry, breaks shields, and charges the flag. Its only real weakness is archers, who shred it — which is exactly why it cannot be the top of the meta. |
| **Spear Militia** | Spear (2★) | Strong for a 2-star. More mobility and range than Shieldman, which makes it better at dirty placements. One creator put it in S, most put it in A. |
| **[Samurai](/units/samurai/)** | Special (4★) | High damage with more bulk than Berserker, and it beats any cavalry in prolonged combat. The safer of the two 4-star damage carries. |
| **Pikeman** | Spear | Rated A by creators who value the Spearman buffs carrying up the evolution line, and C by those who say Halberdier does the same job better. Read the [Pikeman note](#where-creators-disagree) before investing. |
| **Imperial Halberdier** | Spear (4★) | A tankier Halberdier with more damage. Everything good about Halberdier, one star higher. |
| **[Immortal](/units/immortal/)** | Special (4★) | Durable follow-up unit. The Lancer breaks the line, the Immortal walks through the gap — and it is no slower than the horse in practice. |

## B tier

| Unit | Type | Why it's B-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shieldman** | Shield (1★) | Slow, but genuinely tanky — the best beginner frontline and the start of the Imperial Wall line. No offensive output, which is the whole point. |
| **Sentinel** | Shield (2★) | Tanky 2-star with stats close to Spear Militia. A solid mid-step on the way to Imperial Wall. |
| **Cavalry** | Cavalry (2★) | A beginner-level cavalry. Described as one of the weaker B-tier units — it does the job until you can field a Lancer. |
| **Squire** | Shield (2★) | A stepping stone to Knight. Not a unit you build around. |
| **Mercenary** | Shield (1★) | The other starter shield unit, and the first step toward Knight. Useful early, replaced quickly. |

## C tier

| Unit | Type | Why it's C-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Archer** | Ranged (1★) | Was a core unit; the 1.5 range nerf turned it into a stepping stone. Field it only to evolve into Flame Archers. |
| **Javeliner** | Ranged (2★) | Hyped, then dropped. Creators bumped it up and then walked it back — "no one's using this." It is unusual in that it can break shields, but its block and HP are too low to hold a line. |
| **Pikeman** | Spear | See the disagreement note above — half the sources rank it C. |
| **Reaper** | Special (2★) | Update 2's new unit and a disappointment. Built for one-on-one duels in an army game, and it loses to archers it cannot reach. |

## Where creators disagree

Two units split the sources, and pretending otherwise would make this list less useful.

**Mace.** One creator rates it **S tier** — a melee DPS that breaks blocks, walks through shield lines, and synergises beautifully with your own shield units eating arrows. Another rates it **among the worst shock infantry in the game**. Both agree it has block damage; they disagree on how often that pays off. If you are short on star budget, treat Mace as a specialist you bring when the enemy is shield-heavy, not a default pick.

**Pikeman.** Rated A by sources who see the Spearman buffs carrying up the line, and C by sources who say Halberdier simply does it better.

## How to use this list

Rankings are only half the decision. Three systems constrain what you can actually field:

1. **Star limit** — every unit costs its star level against a cap that grows with your account level. A 4-star unit eats a third of an early-game budget, which is why creators tell new players to take Spartan and Samurai and skip the rest. The [star limit guide](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) covers the maths.
2. **Counters** — a C-tier unit that hard-counters your S-tier unit wins. [Unit counters](/guides/unit-counters/) explains the chart and how to check block damage in-game.
3. **Evolution stage** — a fully invested 2-star beats a fresh 3-star. Read the [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) before you evolve anything, because evolving raises the star cost.
