This tier list reflects the **consensus across the major Command An Army creator tier lists and strategy guides** from September 2026 — there is no official ranking, and the meta shifts with each balance patch. Rankings are based on how each unit performs in real matches: frontline survival, rush damage, and how badly ranged fire punishes it.

The short version: **Spartans and Lancers** are the two units every source agrees on, Samurai and Fire Archer lead the second tier, and the Berserker is the classic trap — the highest damage in the game that dies before it deals any.

## Who are the S-tier units?

| Unit | Type | Why it's S-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spartan** | Special | The definitive frontline. Shield pushes let them advance into ranged fire without losing health — creators describe them soloing whole unit groups. The default pick for both attack and defense. |
| **Lancer** | Cavalry (3-star) | Fastest unit in the game with the highest rush damage. The Scout Rider line's final form, and the reason that line is worth investing in. |

Spartans are also the yardstick other units are measured against — one widely-shared line from the tier list videos is that shield units can solo "everything except Spartans". If you pull one, build around it.

## Who sits in A-tier?

| Unit | Type | Why it's A-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Samurai** | Special | Strong damage with more bulk than the Berserker — creators rank it A, with some putting it at S. The safer of the two damage carries. |
| **Fire Archer** | Ranged (3-star) | The top ranged 3-star and the payoff of the Archer → Longbowman → Fire Archer line. Ranged units decide fights in this game; this is the best one. |
| **Halberdier** | Spears | The recommended defensive pick when you don't have Spartans — spears behind shields blunt cavalry rushes. |

## Who is the B-tier (and why)?

| Unit | Type | The catch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Berserker** | Special | Highest damage in the game, but "takes way too much damage from ranged projectiles" — creators consistently rank it below Samurai because it melts before it connects. Use behind a shield wall or not at all. |
| **Crossbowman** | Ranged (3-star) | A budget Fire Archer: same role, noticeably weaker, much easier to pull. Fine while you chase the real thing. |
| **Mace** | Melee (3-star) | A solid three-star melee unit — creators rate it a role player rather than a carry. |
| **Shieldman line** (Sentinel, Imperial Wall) | Heavy Shields | Not flashy, but defense objectives are won by shields. Every evolution of this line earns its slot in a defending formation. |

## How should the tier list change how I play?

Three practical rules that come out of every strategy guide:

1. **Build two or three types, not one.** Type matchups beat raw rarity — a mono-cavalry army loses to a spear wall no matter the stars. The [units guide](/units/all-units/) maps every type and what it counters.
2. **Ascend and master what you have.** A fully ascended, mastery-capped 2-star outperforms a fresh 3-star in its role. Duplicates are not junk.
3. **Match the unit to the objective.** Defense favors shields and spears (Imperial Wall, Halberdier); attack favors rush units (Lancer). Switching loadouts between rounds is the cheapest upgrade available.
