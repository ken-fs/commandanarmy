This list combines **two independent sources** and tells you where they disagree instead of averaging it away:

- **Eurogamer's tier list**, published **October 12, 2026** — it tiers each line by its final evolved form and weighs how easy the unit is to actually get.
- **A creator tier list recorded on October 17, 2026** — after the class-expansion update, ranked by what wins at max investment in the live build.

Neither source has been rebuilt for the **October 19 balance patch**, so the movements that patch caused are called out separately below.

If you are still learning the systems behind the rankings, read [unit counters](/guides/unit-counters/) first — a well-countered S-tier unit still loses to a C-tier unit that hard-counters it.

## S tier

| Unit | Type | Why it's S-tier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Phalangite](/units/phalangite/)** | Spear (3★) | Both sources rank it first. Eurogamer calls it "easily the most broken in the game"; creator testing calls it the best defensive unit and "one of the best max upgraded units so far." The October 19 block nerf (45 → 30) does not change the ranking — the reach and the circle formation are what win. |
| **Flame Archer** | Ranged (3★) | The best ranged unit and the payoff of the [Archer line](/units/archer/). Arched shots plus ignite mean it can fire from positions nothing else can reach. Eurogamer ranks it S; creator testing puts it as the one unit above the Phalangite on flexibility. |
| **[Lancer](/units/lancer/)** | Cavalry (4★) | The fastest unit in the game and the highest rush damage. Creator testing calls it the best all-rounder outright; Eurogamer's S tier is for the whole Scout Rider line. If you have no spears, a Lancer is how you lose a point. |
| **[Samurai](/units/samurai/)** | Special (4★) | The one unit rated S by both sources. Eurogamer: "one of the more slept-on units… a pretty solid hard counter to the Laestrygonian." Creator testing is blunter — Samurai beats the Berserker, and its parry is why. |
| **[Laestrygonian](/units/laestrygonian/)** | Giant (5★) | Highest damage in the game. Slow and nine per squad, so it needs a shield in front, but nothing else deletes a formation as fast. Its counter is the spear wall, which is why the Phalangite is above it. |

## What changed after October 17

Three things moved the board since most tier lists were written:

1. **Two new melee player classes and one new unit.** The [Poleaxe and Archer classes](/guides/mongol-update/) are loadout weapons, not units, so they do not take a tier here. The **[Pavise Crossbowman](/units/pavise-crossbowman/)** does: highest B.
2. **October 19 rebalance.** Berserker up (16 → 18 units, damage 29 → 33, DPS 44); Phalangite down (block 45 → 30). The Berserker is now the highest-DPS 4-star melee pick on paper, but it still loses to anything that outlasts its first clash.
3. **The Rapier question.** After the Berserker's damage went up, creator testing pointed at the 2-star Rapier as the actual one-on-one answer — 15% parry base, 30% at three stars, plus skill tree. That is a real counter the older lists do not account for.

## Where the sources disagree

**Rapier.** Eurogamer puts it in C and says the hard-counter risk outweighs the support DPS — "best left on the bench." Creator testing calls it the most underrated unit in the game and says it beats three-star units at parry-heavy trades. The disagreement is about **how often the parry lands in a real match**; if you play duels and flanks, test it yourself before discarding it.

**Immortal.** Eurogamer has it A as a quick flanker that spawns in numbers; creator testing rates it so low that a single cavalry mistake wipes the squad. Same unit, opposite conclusions — B is the honest answer until a third source shows numbers.

**Spearman and Archer.** Eurogamer ranks both lines S because it tiers the evolved form. Creator testing ranks the base form C or D. Both are right: the 1-star you field today is a stepping stone, and the line you are investing in is top tier.

## How to use this list

Rankings are only half the decision. Three systems constrain what you can actually field:

1. **Star limit** — every unit costs its star level against a cap that grows with your account level. A 4-star unit eats a third of an early-game budget, which is why creators tell new players to take Spartan and Samurai and skip the rest. The [star limit guide](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) covers the maths.
2. **Counters** — a C-tier unit that hard-counters your S-tier unit wins. [Unit counters](/guides/unit-counters/) explains the chart and how to check block damage in-game.
3. **Evolution stage** — a fully invested 2-star beats a fresh 3-star. Read the [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) before you evolve anything, because evolving raises the star cost.
