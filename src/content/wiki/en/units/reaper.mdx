## What the Reaper is

**Reaper** is one of two units added in **Update 2**, alongside the [Rapier](/guides/update-2/). It is a **2-star special unit**, and the creator verdict after testing is unusually blunt: it is one of the weakest units in the game.

That is worth stating plainly because summons are the scarce resource in Command An Army, and this is a page whose most useful advice is "do not spend them here."

## Why it does not work

The problem is not a number that could be patched. It is a design mismatch.

**Reaper is built for one-on-one duels.** Command An Army is an army-versus-army game — creators describe the core loop as team-vs-team pushes into capture points. A unit whose value is winning a solo fight does not have a job in that structure. One tier list put it memorably: it is an army game, not a one-on-one game.

**It loses to archers it cannot reach.** The same weakness every melee unit has, without the durability of a shield or the speed of a [Lancer](/units/scout-rider/) to survive the approach. In a meta where **Flame Archer is S tier**, that is close to fatal — the most common damage source in the game beats it for free.

**It costs stars for nothing.** At 2 stars it is not cheap in budget terms, and the [star limit](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) means every weak unit you field is a star not spent on the unit that would have won the fight.

## What to do instead

If you want a damage unit, the tier list is unambiguous: **Berserker** and **Samurai** at 4 stars, **Flame Archer** at 3. If you want something cheap that holds a line while your carry works, the [Shieldman line](/units/shieldman/) starts at 1 star.

If you are specifically interested in what Update 2 added, the **Rapier** is the more interesting of the two new units — a 2-star that evolves into a **Rapier Knight** with a parry, which is a genuinely new defensive mechanic rather than a duelist in an army game. The [Update 2 guide](/guides/update-2/) covers it.

## A note on new-unit hype

Reaper is a useful case study for how to read this game's patch notes, because it shows the difference between **new** and **good**.

Update 2 was a mode patch — Ranked and Guilds — with two units attached. Creators expect the next update to be unit-focused, and that is when the meta will actually move. A new unit in a mode patch is a bonus, not a balance event, and the two are worth judging separately.

The [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) is rebuilt whenever the meta moves, which in practice means the 1.5-style balance patches rather than the mode patches.
