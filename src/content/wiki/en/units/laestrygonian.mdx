The **Laestrygonian** is Command An Army's first **5-star unit**, added in the **Mongol update** in early October 2026. It's a squad of nine giants that hit really hard and splash damage onto anything nearby. It's strong, but only when something else is taking the hits for it.

## What does the Laestrygonian do?

It's a heavy damage unit. You get **nine giants**, each one big and tanky, and each swing hits the main target plus enemies around it.

Here's what creators measured. Your own numbers will differ with level, [Ascension](/units/all-units/) (merging duplicate copies) and the skill tree.

| Stat | What testers saw |
| --- | --- |
| Squad size | 9 |
| Base HP | 700 (fresh copy) |
| Base damage | 120 (fresh copy) |
| Main hit at Ascension 3 | about 160–190 |
| Splash at Ascension 3 | about 80–90, on up to 4 nearby enemies |

The takeaway: each giant hits like a small squad, but there are very few of them.

The splash used to hit three enemies. A buff after launch raised it to **four**. One tester says it breaks enemy blocks "in one shot", which makes it a real answer to shield lines.

## Why do some players say it's bad?

Because it dies fast when you use it wrong. The giants are huge, so everything can hit them. With only nine of them, a crowd of enemies can shred the squad before the splash pays off.

It was also weaker at launch, before the splash buff. A lot of the early "it's trash" takes came from that version.

Losing the whole squad hurts, too. One creator noted a wait of about 100 seconds before he could bring his giants back.

## How should you use it?

Keep it **behind a tank**. Testers pair it with [Imperial Wall](/units/shieldman/) (the last form of the Shieldman line) or another shield unit that holds the point. Let the tank soak the hits while the giants swing into the packed enemy line.

A few simple rules:

- Don't send it in first. Let your shields make contact, then bring the giants in.
- Pull it back before the squad is wiped. A half-dead squad that survives is better than a long respawn.
- Aim it at shield units. Breaking blocks is where it does the most damage.

## Where does it rank?

One creator who maxed every Mongol-update unit puts it in **S tier** for damage and shock trooping. He says he may be a bit biased, since his copy is Ascension 3. Other players rate it much lower, mostly because of how fast it dies when used alone.

Our read: it's a great unit if you already own a solid shield tank. If you don't, a cheap tank will help your army more right now.
