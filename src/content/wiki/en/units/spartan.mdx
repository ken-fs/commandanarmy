The **Spartan is the best unit in Command An Army** as of the Guild & Ranked update — every creator tier list from September 2026 puts it in the top tier, and strategy guides build their default plan around it. What makes it special is the shield advance: a Spartan walks into ranged fire without losing health, which is the exact thing that kills every other unit on approach. If you pull one, you build around it. This page covers what it does, how to get it, and how to use it.

## What does the Spartan actually do?

The Spartan is a **shield unit** — creators call it "the main shield guy" — whose defining trait is that its advance absorbs ranged fire. In a game where ranged units decide fights, that one property flips the core matchup: instead of your frontline dying before it connects, the Spartan connects first and stays alive.

Strategy videos show the result directly: a formation of Spartans pushing a capture point while the enemy's lancers are "taken out" — the Spartan line survives the exchange nearly intact. One widely-cited creator line is that shield units can solo groups of rare units, with the Spartan as the shield unit nothing else beats.

## How do I get a Spartan?

**Summons only.** The Spartan is a high-rarity pull from the unit summon pool — there is no quest, shop, or milestone route to it, which is why it appears in every "what should I roll for" discussion.

The practical path:

1. Redeem every working code — the current set pays **3,500+ Gems**, roughly 60–70 summons ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/))
2. Summon in bulk rather than single rolls — the first big batch is where your roster comes from
3. Keep duplicates of everything you field; **Ascension** merges two copies of the same unit into a stronger version

The [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) walks through the first-hour version of this routine.

## How should I use the Spartan?

The consensus formation puts the Spartan first and everything else behind it:

| Position | Unit | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frontline | **Spartan** | Walks through ranged fire; holds capture points almost alone |
| Behind the line | Ranged (Fire Archer line, Crossbowman) | Fires over the shield wall — the Spartan's advance buys them free uptime |
| Flank / flex | Lancer or other cavalry | Punishes enemies who retreat from the Spartan push |

Two tactical notes from strategy guides:

- **Order it to hold a point, not to chase.** A Spartan holding a chokepoint is worth several Spartans wandering after kills — use the formation commands (number keys for hold, advance to push).
- **Don't swap it out.** One creator's summary of their winning setup: "you don't really need to equip anything else — all I need is the Spartan." If you don't have one, defensive spears (Halberdier) and the Shieldman line are the recommended stand-ins.

## What beats a Spartan?

Honestly: not much, and nothing cleanly. Creators frame the counter question the other way around — shield units solo other units, and the Spartan is the shield unit that beats the rest. The realistic answers are:

- **Volume**: two or three strong units focus-firing one Spartan while it is committed
- **Avoidance**: don't fight the Spartan's push — take a different capture point and make it walk
- **Its own rarity**: Spartans are rare pulls, so most lobbies have one at most; the games you lose are usually games where the enemy has one and you don't
