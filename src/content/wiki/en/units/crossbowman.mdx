The **Crossbowman** is a ranged unit you'll probably pull early and bench later. It's not bad. It just gets outclassed once your Archer line is grown up.

## How do I get a Crossbowman?

**Summon it.** It's a **3-star** unit in the standard summon banner with about a **1% base chance** per pull, per the community wiki. Gems come from quests, milestones and [codes](/codes/all-codes/).

It's **not part of the Archer line**. You can't evolve an Archer into it, and it's not a step toward the Flame Archer. The community wiki lists an evolved form, the **Pavise Crossbowman**, but we haven't found a second source or any test numbers for it yet, so treat that one as unconfirmed.

## What it's good at

Range. The Crossbowman shoots further than the base Archer, which makes it a nice pull in the early game, when your Archers are still level 1 and weak.

Eurogamer's take (October 12, 2026) is that it "still can do serious damage" and is "much easier to obtain than the likes of the Fire Archer or the Elite Javelineer." If you need ranged damage right now and don't want to grind an evolution line, it gets the job done.

## Why it falls off

Same source, other half of the sentence: it's "probably the worst of the ranged units you can currently get," mostly because it's **slower and less versatile**. The community wiki agrees that the base unit isn't great for a 3-star, and suggests that once you're out of the early game, you either evolve it or drop it.

Compare it to the other ranged options:

| Unit | How you get it | Strength | Tier (Eurogamer) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Flame Archer](/units/archer/) | Evolve Archer → Longbowman → Flame Archer | Fire damage, best ranged unit | S (via Archer) |
| [Elite Javelineer](/units/javelineer/) | Summon Javelineer, then evolve | Beats shields, has block | A (via Javelineer) |
| [Khorchin](/units/khorchin/) | Mongol battle pass | Mounted, can flee and reposition | A |
| **Crossbowman** | Summon (about 1%) | Range, no grind | B |

## What beats it

- **Cavalry.** Every ranged unit loses to horses that reach it. A [Lancer](/units/lancer/) charge deletes it.
- **Better ranged units.** Flame Archers and Elite Javelineers win the long-range trade.

## When to use it

- **Early game.** If you pulled one, use it while your Archer line levels up.
- **As a backup shooter.** It's fine filler behind a frontline while your main units respawn.
- **Not as your main ranged unit** once you have a Flame Archer. Spend the slot and the stars on that instead. Your [star limit](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) is tight, and a 3-star B-tier unit is an expensive bench warmer.

## Where it sits

B tier: handy early, outclassed later. See the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) and [all units](/units/all-units/) for the rest of the roster.
