## What the Immortal is

Immortal is a **4-star special unit** in the same rarity band as Spartan, Samurai, Lancer and Berserker. It is not the fastest, not the tankiest and not the highest damage — its role is **follow-up**.

## The Lancer pairing

The clearest explanation of what the Immortal does comes from how creators use it alongside cavalry.

A [Lancer](/units/scout-rider/) is a three-second unit: it breaks a line in one shock charge and then has nothing left. The problem with a pure cavalry push is that the gap closes again as soon as the horse is dead.

The Immortal solves that. Creators describe the pairing as **Lancer breaks the line, Immortal walks through the gap** — the horse creates the opening, the durable unit holds it long enough for the rest of the army to arrive.

There is a detail that makes the pairing work better than expected: creators note the Immortal is **no slower than a Lancer in practice**, despite being far more durable. That means it can actually keep up with a cavalry push rather than arriving after the fight is decided.

## What it trades away

The Immortal is durable and mobile, which means it gives up burst.

It does not break a line the way a Lancer does. It does not tank a volley the way a Spartan does. It does not out-damage a Berserker or a Samurai.

That is why it sits in **A tier** rather than S on the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/). It is not a unit you build an army around — it is the unit that converts another unit's opening into a won fight.

## How to use it

**Send it second, not first.** The Immortal follows a charge; it does not make one. Sending it in alone spends a 4-star unit to achieve nothing.

**Use the durability, not the damage.** Its value in a fight is that it stays alive in the middle of the enemy formation while your other units do their work. Position it where you need something to still be standing in ten seconds.

**Pair it with the cavalry line.** The [Scout Rider line](/units/scout-rider/) produces the Lancer that makes the Immortal's job possible. Read them together.

**Respect the star cost.** At 4 stars it competes directly with Spartan, Samurai, Lancer and Berserker for your budget. On a small account, the [star limit guide](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/) will usually tell you to field one of those and fill the rest cheaply.

## Where it sits

Immortal is A tier as of September 2026.
