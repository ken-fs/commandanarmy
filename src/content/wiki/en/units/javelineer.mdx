The **Javelineer** is the odd one out among ranged units. It has the shortest range, but it can hurt shields and it can take a hit. It's a 2-star summon that evolves into the **Elite Javelineer**.

## How do I get a Javelineer?

**Summon it.** It's in the standard summon banner at about a **4% base chance** per pull, according to the community wiki. Redeem the current [codes](/codes/all-codes/) for Gems first.

For the **Elite Javelineer**, level the Javelineer to **20** and put it on the **Evolve podium**, across from the Ascension area. The [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) explains the process.

| Stage | Unit | Star | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Javelineer | 2★ | Ranged, has block, good against shields |
| 2 | **Elite Javelineer** | 3★ | Same strengths, much better block |

It's a separate line from the [Archer](/units/archer/) line. You can't turn a Javelineer into a Flame Archer.

## What makes it different

The community wiki calls out two traits that no other ranged unit has, except Flame Archers:

1. **It's good against shields.** That makes it a real answer to [Spartans](/units/spartan/) and [Imperial Walls](/units/shieldman/), which normally shrug off arrows.
2. **It has block.** Most ranged units have none. Javelineers can defend themselves a bit, and on the Elite Javelineer that block gets much better. The wiki says it shows when it trades with Archers and Crossbowmen.

The catch is **range**. It has the shortest range of the ranged units, so it often has to stand closer to the fight than you'd like.

## Why tier lists disagree

You'll see this unit everywhere from C to A:

- **Earlier creator lists** dropped it to **C** after an early hype wave didn't hold up.
- **Eurogamer (October 12, 2026)** has it in **A tier**. They point out it has been buffed since launch, which made the Elite Javelineer "quite the unit to be trifled with," especially against melee enemies in the right spot.

Our read: the buffs are real, but it's still a specialist. Bring it when the enemy leans on shields. For general ranged damage, the [Flame Archer](/units/archer/) is still better, because its fire keeps chipping away at targets.

## What beats it

- **Cavalry.** The wiki says it plainly: rushing cavalry counters it. A [Lancer](/units/lancer/) that reaches your javelins wins.
- **Long-range shooters.** Archers outrange it, so on open maps it can get picked apart before it's in range.

## How to use it

- **Point it at shields.** Its job is to soften a Spartan or Imperial Wall push.
- **Keep spears in front of it.** A [Halberdier](/units/halberdier/) or a Spearman line stops the cavalry that would otherwise delete it.
- **Use the block.** It can hold a spot that a plain archer squad can't, so it's fine closer to the front than other ranged units.

## Where it sits

A shield-counter specialist that went from C to A as it got buffed. See the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) and the [unit counters guide](/guides/unit-counters/) for how it fits.
