The **Khorchin** is a **4-star horse archer** from Command An Army's **Mongol update** (early October 2026). It's fast and hard to pin down, but it hits weakly and Fire Archers outrange it. Short answer: fun, but not worth chasing.

## What does the Khorchin do?

It's a mounted archer squad. It can shoot from range, then run away when enemies come close. Normal archers can't do that.

| Stat | What testers saw |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Horse archer (mounted ranged) |
| Squad size | 15 (Fire Archers have 20) |
| Range | 257 (Fire Archer: 321, same test) |
| Damage per second | 11.2 at level 7 (his Fire Archers: 10.8, about 12 levels higher) |
| How to get it | Battle pass |

The takeaway: the damage per second looks fine for a low level, but you get fewer riders and less range than Fire Archers.

One tester who maxed every new unit goes further. He says it has the **lowest damage and lowest HP in the game**.

## What's it good at?

**Staying alive.** In one creator's test, enemies charged his Khorchins and only three died. He says a line of normal archers would have lost about half.

It also works best on **big open maps**. Plains and the new mountain map give it room to run circles around slow enemies.

## What's bad about it?

- **Fire Archers beat it.** They outrange it and they're one of the most used units right now. One creator said the Khorchin would be much better if Fire Archers weren't so strong.
- **Shield meta.** Shield units sit on a point and soak arrows. A Khorchin barely hurts them.
- **Bad on tight maps.** On Castle the horses get stuck on everything. One creator says it's the worst map for them, especially on defense.
- **It takes micro.** You have to keep moving it. Leave it alone and it does very little.

## Where does it rank?

One creator puts it at **B tier**: "above average, but still a bit mediocre". Another puts it at **low A**: "a really annoying fly", but nothing crazy. The B-tier creator thinks it needs a buff, or Fire Archers need a nerf.

Our read: if you already have [Fire Archers](/units/archer/), stick with them. Don't grind the battle pass just for the Khorchin.

It came out with the 5-star [Laestrygonian](/units/laestrygonian/) and the [Myrmidon](/units/myrmidon/). For the wider meta, see the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/).
