The **Samurai is Command An Army's balanced damage carry** — ranked A or S tier by every creator list, with damage creators place second only to the Berserker and far more survivability. The comparison that defines it: the Berserker hits harder but "takes way too much damage from ranged projectiles", while the Samurai is bulkier and survives the approach. In a game decided by ranged fire, surviving is what converts damage into wins.

## Why is the Samurai ranked above the Berserker?

Because the meta punishes glass cannons. Creator tier list commentary makes the trade explicit:

| | Berserker | Samurai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Raw damage | Highest in the game | Second to the Berserker |
| Survivability | Melts to ranged projectiles | Notably bulkier |
| Practical result | Dies before connecting | Reaches the fight and delivers |

The tier list verdict: the Berserker lands lower despite its damage numbers, while the Samurai sits at **A tier, with some creators putting it at S**. If your formation can perfectly shelter a Berserker, it pays off; for everyone else, the Samurai is the pick that works without perfect play.

## How do I use the Samurai?

The Samurai is a **second-line damage unit**:

1. **Let the frontline start the fight.** Shields or spears engage first — the Samurai commits after the enemy is fixed in place, not before.
2. **Stay near your ranged units.** The Samurai protects them from melee pressure while they whittle the enemy line; in exchange, their fire covers the Samurai's approach.
3. **Focus the break.** When the enemy line buckles, the Samurai is the unit that converts a push into a capture — direct it at the point, not at stragglers.
4. **Ascend and master it.** Duplicates merge through Ascension, and Mastery levels a unit to 20 — a fully invested Samurai outclasses a fresh higher-star pull in its role.

## How do I get a Samurai?

Samurai is a **summon-pool pull** — no quest or shop route. The routine is the same as for any high-rarity unit:

1. Redeem every working code — the current set pays **3,500+ Gems**, roughly 60–70 summons ([codes page](/codes/all-codes/))
2. Roll in bulk, not single pulls
3. Keep duplicates of anything you field — they feed Ascension, not the sell button
