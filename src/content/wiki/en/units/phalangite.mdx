The **Phalangite** is the end of the Spearman line, and right now it's the unit everyone is talking about. It came with the [Mongol update](/guides/mongol-update/) in early October 2026. Eurogamer's tier list (October 12) calls it "easily the most broken in the game right now."

## How do I get the Phalangite?

You can't pull it. You **evolve** it, the same way you get a Lancer or a Flame Archer:

| Stage | Unit | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Spearman | Starter unit, also in summons |
| 2 | Pikeman | Evolve a level 20 Spearman |
| 3 | **Phalangite** | Evolve a level 20 Pikeman |

To evolve, get the unit to **level 20** by using it in matches, then go to the **Evolve podium**, across from the Ascension area, and drop it in. If it can evolve, the next form shows up on the right. The [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) has every line.

One thing to watch: sources don't agree on its star cost. The community wiki's unit list shows 3 stars, its update notes say 4. Check before you plan around it, because a 4-star Phalangite fights a Spartan or a Samurai for the same slot under your [star limit](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/).

## Why it's so strong

It comes down to **reach**. Phalangite spears are much longer than the Pikeman's, so it hits first, and most melee units take damage the whole way in. One creator who ran it on defense said the enemy "couldn't even get close" to the formation.

The other half is the **circle formation**. Put Phalangites in a circle on a point and there is no soft side to flank. That's where it shines:

- It shuts down **cavalry rushes**. Spears already beat horses, and a longer spear beats them earlier.
- It out-trades **shield turtles**. A Spartan turtle's small circle takes hits from many spearmen at once.
- It beats other spears. In a test, a Spear Vanguard circle never landed a hit.

## What still beats it

Creator Tuki ran a stress test (October 8, 2026): 9 fights against 6 different units, attacking and defending. The Phalangite **won 8 and lost 1**:

| Opponent | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Lancer rush into a Phalangite circle | Phalangite wins, no losses |
| Spartan turtle vs Phalangite circle | Phalangite wins, 15 of 28 left |
| Spear Vanguard circle (both Ascension 3) | Phalangite wins, 28 of 28 left |
| Phalangite attacking a Spartan turtle | Phalangite wins |
| Phalangite attacking a Spear Vanguard circle | Phalangite wins |
| Spear Vanguard, straight brawl | Phalangite wins |
| Knight (Ascension 3) | Phalangite wins |
| [Myrmidon](/units/myrmidon/) | Phalangite wins |
| [Laestrygonian](/units/laestrygonian/) (level 19, not ascended) vs max Phalangite | **Laestrygonian wins, no losses** |

So the answer is the **Laestrygonian**. Its cleave hits several spearmen per swing, which is exactly what a packed spear wall can't handle. If you're defending a point against Phalangites, the creator's advice was blunt: spam Laestrygonians.

Ranked players saw the same thing. Another creator's ranked matches had Phalangite plus Laestrygonian as the new meta pair. On defense nobody got inside the spear wall; on attack their team lost twice to the same Phalangite turtle.

## How to use it

- **Defend with it.** It's at its best sitting in a circle on a point. Make the enemy come to you.
- **Pair it with something that handles giants.** The Laestrygonian is its one bad matchup. Eurogamer calls the [Samurai](/units/samurai/) a hard counter to Laestrygonians, so it covers the gap.
- **Don't skip the Spearman.** The 1.5 patch already made the base Spearman faster with 28 units per formation, so the line is worth playing while you level it.
- **Spear lines are separate.** The Phalangite comes only from Spearman. The [Halberdier](/units/halberdier/) and [Spear Militia](/units/spear-militia/) are their own lines.

Top of the current meta, with one clear counter. For how it fits with the rest of the roster, see the [best units tier list](/guides/best-units-tier-list/) and the [unit counters guide](/guides/unit-counters/).
