The **Knight** is what a Mercenary turns into if you stick with it. It isn't a meta unit, but it's a solid shock troop, and the line starts from a starter unit, so any account can work toward it.

## How do I get a Knight?

You **evolve** it from the Mercenary, one of the starter units:

| Stage | Unit | Star | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mercenary | 1★ | Starter melee unit. The weakest of the three 1-star starters. |
| 2 | Squire | 2★ | Fairly high DPS, usable as a shock unit, still weaker than most melee. |
| 3 | **Knight** | 3★ | 24 per squad, high DPS, more block. The payoff. |

Each step works the same way: level the unit to **20** by using it in matches, then put it on the **Evolve podium**, across from the Ascension area. The [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) covers every line, and each stage costs one more star in your lineup, so check your [star limit](/guides/star-limit-and-lineup/).

## What the Knight is good at

The community wiki sums up the Knight as "superior in every way" to its earlier forms, and mostly used as a **shock unit**. That means you hit an enemy line hard and fast, usually from the side, while your frontline holds them in place.

Two things make it work:

- **24 units per squad.** Bigger squads absorb more damage and trade better. With that count, Knights can go head-on with other shock units like the [Berserker](/units/berserker/) and the [Immortal](/units/immortal/), and the wiki says they can solo most other units if you use them well.
- **More block.** Arrows hurt it less than they hurt the Squire, so a few archers won't just melt it on the way in.

## What beats it

- **Cavalry.** A charging [Lancer](/units/lancer/) or Scout Rider is the cleanest counter. Don't leave Knights out in the open when the enemy has horses.
- **Ranged units.** [Flame Archers](/units/archer/) and Javelineers still do real damage to it, block or not.
- **The current meta.** In one creator's test, an Ascension 3 Knight lost to the [Phalangite](/units/phalangite/). Its long spears hit first and the Knights never made contact.

## Is it worth the grind?

Honestly, it's a middle pick. Eurogamer rates the line **B tier**: "a decent mid-tier all-rounder," and good filler to keep on the field while your bigger 4-star units respawn. It takes two full evolutions to get there, which is a lot of matches for a B-tier unit.

Our take:

- **Do it if** you already have a frontline and a damage carry, and want a cheap 3-star to fill the last slots.
- **Skip it for now if** you're still building your first real lineup. A Spearman you can push to [Phalangite](/units/phalangite/), or an Archer you can push to Flame Archer, pays off more for the same leveling time.
