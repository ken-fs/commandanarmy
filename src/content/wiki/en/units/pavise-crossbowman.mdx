The **Pavise Crossbowman** is the **4-star** end of the Crossbowman line, added in the **October 17, 2026** class-expansion update. It is the first ranged unit in the game with a shield, and the verdict from the creators who took it to level 20 is consistent: a real upgrade to a weak unit, still not a unit you build around.

## How do I get a Pavise Crossbowman?

**Evolve it.** There is no summon route:

1. Pull a **Crossbowman** — a 3-star from the standard banner, about **1%** per pull ([how the Crossbowman works](/units/crossbowman/)).
2. Level it to **20**.
3. Evolve it on the Evolve podium for the 4-star **Pavise Crossbowman**.

The [evolution guide](/guides/evolution-guide/) covers the podium and star costs. It is **not** part of the Archer line — Archers go Archer → Longbowman → Flame Archer and never reach this unit. If you already own a levelled Crossbowman, the evolution is the cheapest 4-star you will get this month; if you do not, summoning and levelling one just for this is a poor use of gems ([get them from codes](/codes/all-codes/) first).

Two readings float around, so treat the ceiling as approximate: one creator read the freshly evolved unit as **12.1 DPS at 270 range with 115 block**, and a second creator's maxed copy showed **24.2 DPS and 230 damage** with range nodes pushing it toward **300**. The lower number is the base card; the higher one includes upgrades. We have not seen a third source confirm which stage that 24.2 reading came from.

## What the shield actually changes

**Survivability.** 115 block on a ranged unit is the whole point of the pavise. Against archers and other ranged chip damage it lives noticeably longer than the base Crossbowman, and inside a spear or shield line it survives the first volley that used to kill it.

Stay disciplined about what that buys: it does **not** make the unit tanky. Lancers still reach it and still delete it — a creator testing it against the current meta put it plainly: "even with a shield, their biggest enemy, lancers, still just wipe them out in seconds."

## What it can't do

**It cannot arc shots.** Every Archer-line unit lobs over walls and formations; the Pavise Crossbowman fires flat. That removes the hiding spots archers rely on — behind a wall, on a ledge, on high ground — and it means you have to keep it in the open with a frontline in front of it, which is exactly where cavalry wants to find it.

That single limitation is why the unit is rated mid despite carrying a shield that no other ranged unit has.

## Is it worth it?

Creator consensus after testing at level 20+ is **no, not as a goal** — yes, if it falls out of a Crossbowman you already levelled.

The blunt version from [DinozaurPlays](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HDSPYiwCJSs) (October 17): "the better version of an already bad unit… makes it better, but the better version of an already bad unit doesn't have to be great… I would not spend your time grinding for it." He rates it better than the Crossbowman, worse than the Flame Archer, and specifically worse than the [Longbowman](/units/archer/) line you get on the way to the Flame Archer.

Where that leaves it in the ranged queue:

| Unit | How you get it | Still better because |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Flame Archer](/units/archer/) | Evolve Archer → Longbowman → Flame Archer | Arched shots, ignite damage, best ranged unit |
| [Elite Javelineer](/units/javelineer/) | Evolve a Javelineer | Shield-breaking damage, block of its own |
| [Khorchin](/units/khorchin/) | Mongol battle pass | Mounted, can reposition and flee |
| **Pavise Crossbowman** | Evolve a level 20 Crossbowman | 115 block and 270 range, no arcing, slow |

## Skill tree: what creators picked

Skill crystals go into the same tree as the Crossbowman ([how the tree works](/guides/mongol-update/)). The path creators tested: **accuracy** and **precision** first for the flat-shot problem, then **heart seeker**, **damage**, **range** and **speed**, with **piercing** as the last pick. Range nodes are what took the maxed copy from 270 to about 300.

If you only have crystals for one branch, take **range** — the unit's problem is not damage, it is finding a firing position it can actually shoot from.

## How to beat it

- **Cavalry.** [Lancers](/units/lancer/) reach it through or around a shield line and end it. This holds at every level tested.
- **Anything that forces it into the open.** It needs line of sight, so pushing the fight off its chosen angle costs it most of its value.
- **High ground.** It cannot shoot up a wall. Archers can.
